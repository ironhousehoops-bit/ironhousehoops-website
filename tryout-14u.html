Tryout Details

Everything you need to know before you come in.

Date Saturday, January 2, 2027
Time 5:45–6:45 PM (arrive 15 minutes early for check-in)
Location Brooklyn Park Community Center, 5600 85th Ave N, Minneapolis, MN 55443
What to Bring Your own basketball, basketball shoes, comfortable practice clothes, and a water bottle.
Cost $30 per player, paid online when you register.

The 2027 Season

Every Ironhouse Hoops team, 13u through 17u, gets the same full season.

April–July

A full spring and summer AAU season.

2 Practices a Week

Every team practices twice a week.

6 Guaranteed Tournaments

1 Out-of-State Tournament

Every team gets a chance to compete outside Minnesota.

$850 per Player

The season fee, the same for every team.
